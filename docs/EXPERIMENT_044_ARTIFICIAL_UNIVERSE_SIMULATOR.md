# Experiment 044 — Artificial Universe Simulator

## Goal

Explore a space of possible material worlds by varying interaction laws, environments, and transition rules.

## Core hypothesis

Instead of searching only for materials inside one fixed physics model, investigate how different rule systems generate different classes of self-organization.

\[
Universe = f(Laws, Materials, Environment, History)
\]

## Architecture

```text
Rule set
   ↓
Material population
   ↓
Environment
   ↓
Dynamics simulation
   ↓
Emergent structures
   ↓
Causal analysis
```

## Research questions

- Which rule sets produce stable self-organization?
- Are there universal attractors across different simulated worlds?
- Do simple laws generate complex structures?
- Can AI discover new classes of transition grammar?

## Metrics

- complexity;
- stability;
- adaptability;
- causal compressibility;
- diversity of emergent structures.

## Relation to MorphoMatter

Extends discovery from material search to exploration of possible physics:

\[
observe \rightarrow simulate \rightarrow discover \rightarrow compare worlds
\]
