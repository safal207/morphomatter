# Experiment 043 — Evolution of Causal Laws

## Goal

Move from evolving materials and attractors to studying whether the transition laws themselves can evolve or be discovered.

## Core hypothesis

A system may have multiple possible causal descriptions. The goal is to discover compact, transferable transition laws:

\[
Law_{t+1}=Evolution(Law_t, Evidence)
\]

## Architecture

```text
Observations
      ↓
Causal graph
      ↓
Candidate laws
      ↓
Predictions
      ↓
Falsification tests
      ↓
Improved law
```

## Research questions

- Can simpler laws replace more complex descriptions?
- Can a discovered law transfer between different materials?
- Do universal transition rules emerge?
- Can causal models evolve toward better predictive power?

## Metrics

- prediction accuracy;
- compression of explanations;
- transfer across scales;
- robustness under interventions;
- falsification rate.

## MorphoMatter direction

Evolution is extended from material parameters to explanations themselves:

\[
Material\ Evolution \rightarrow Law\ Evolution
\]
