# Experiment 045 — Evolution of Physics

## Goal

Explore whether artificial universes can evolve their own governing laws through selection, variation, and evidence.

## Core hypothesis

Not only materials and attractors can evolve. Candidate transition laws can also evolve:

\[
Law_{t+1}=Selection(Law_t, Evidence, PredictionError)
\]

## Architecture

```text
Candidate laws
      ↓
Universe simulation
      ↓
Emergent structures
      ↓
Prediction tests
      ↓
Selection
      ↓
Improved laws
```

## Research questions

- Which laws generate stable complexity?
- Do simpler laws dominate under selection?
- Can different initial rules converge to similar principles?
- Are there universal transition laws?

## Metrics

- predictive power;
- description length;
- stability of generated structures;
- transfer across simulated universes;
- robustness under perturbation.

## MorphoMatter direction

The system evolves from discovering materials to exploring possible rule spaces:

\[
Material \rightarrow Attractor \rightarrow Law \rightarrow Universe
\]
