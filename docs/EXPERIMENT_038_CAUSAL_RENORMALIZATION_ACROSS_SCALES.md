# Experiment 038 — Causal Renormalization Across Scales

## Question

Do the causal laws of self-organization remain stable when moving across scales?

Can a transition rule discovered at one scale explain behavior at another?

## Core hypothesis

A complex material may have different microscopic details but preserve higher-level transition grammar:

```
nano
 ↓
micro
 ↓
macro
```

with possible scale-dependent transformations.

## Model

Represent each scale with a causal graph:

```
G_micro(t)
      ↓
G_meso(t)
      ↓
G_macro(t)
```

Compare:

- nodes;
- causal edges;
- delays;
- transition probabilities;
- attractor structure.

## Goals

Find whether there are scale-invariant transition motifs:

```
nucleation
    ↓
growth
    ↓
competition
    ↓
stabilization
```

## Metrics

- causal graph similarity;
- transition grammar compression;
- prediction transfer;
- fractal scaling consistency;
- error growth across scales.

## Research question

Does the material have a universal transition language independent of observation scale?

## Architecture connection

```
Material properties
        ↓
Interaction field
        ↓
Transition grammar
        ↓
Causal graph
        ↓
Scale transformation
        ↓
Universal law discovery
```

## Interpretation boundary

This is a research direction for a synthetic causal simulator. It does not claim real materials share identical scale laws without experimental validation.
