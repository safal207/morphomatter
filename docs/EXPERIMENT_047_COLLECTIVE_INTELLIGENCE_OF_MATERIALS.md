# Experiment 047 — Collective Intelligence of Materials

## Goal

Investigate whether local interactions between simple components can produce collective computation and adaptive behavior.

## Core hypothesis

Global organization can emerge from local rules:

\[
Local\ interactions \rightarrow Global\ computation
\]

The material system may not need direct external control if information processing emerges from its interaction network.

## Architecture

```text
Local particle states
        ↓
Interaction network
        ↓
Collective dynamics
        ↓
Emergent computation
        ↓
Adaptive structure
```

## Research questions

- Can material states encode information?
- Can collective dynamics perform optimization?
- Can defects act as information carriers?
- Can local rules create global problem-solving behavior?

## Metrics

- information capacity;
- collective adaptation speed;
- robustness to perturbations;
- energy cost of computation;
- transfer between environments.

## Relation to MorphoMatter

Extends the project from controlled self-organization toward distributed intelligence:

\[
interaction \rightarrow computation \rightarrow adaptation
\]
