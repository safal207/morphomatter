# Experiment 046 — Observer Effect in Discovery

## Goal

Study how observation strategy changes the space of discovered material laws.

## Core hypothesis

The observer does not change physics directly, but measurement choices influence which patterns become visible and which hypotheses are generated.

\[
Discovery = f(System, Measurements, Questions, History)
\]

## Architecture

```text
Material universe
        ↓
Observation strategy
        ↓
Collected evidence
        ↓
Causal model
        ↓
New experiments
        ↓
Expanded knowledge
```

## Research questions

- Does adaptive observation discover laws faster than random sampling?
- Which measurements reveal hidden causal structure?
- Can an AI choose observations that maximize information gain?
- How does measurement history affect future discovery paths?

## Metrics

- discovery speed;
- information gain;
- causal graph improvement;
- prediction accuracy;
- experiment efficiency.
