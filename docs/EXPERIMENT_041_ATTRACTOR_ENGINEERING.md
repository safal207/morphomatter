# Experiment 041 — Attractor Engineering

## Goal

Move from steering existing transitions to designing and discovering new stable attractor states.

## Core hypothesis

A material system is not only defined by its current states, but by the structure of its attractor landscape:

\[
A = f(Material, Environment, Interaction, History)
\]

Can controlled changes to causal fields create new stable regimes?

## Architecture

```text
Material properties
        ↓
Interaction field Φ(x,t)
        ↓
Energy landscape
        ↓
Attractor candidates
        ↓
Controlled intervention
        ↓
New stable state
```

## Research questions

- Can new attractors appear without changing particle identity?
- Can AI enlarge the reachable state space?
- Are discovered attractors robust under perturbations?
- Do new states have their own transition grammar?

## Metrics

- attractor stability;
- basin of attraction size;
- intervention cost;
- robustness after removing control;
- transfer to new environments.

## Relation to MorphoMatter

This extends the project from transition control to attractor design:

\[
observe \rightarrow understand \rightarrow design \rightarrow stabilize
\]
